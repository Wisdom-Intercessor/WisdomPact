# Charter Annex — Participation, Collective Wisdom and Shared Action

- **Status:** Proposed annex for public review; not yet incorporated into the approved Charter text
- **Version:** 0.1.0
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Parent:** Eternal Wisdom Charter / منشور حکمت جاویدان
- **Companion architecture:** WI-CA-001

## Preamble

The Eternal Wisdom Charter is not intended to remain only a text to be read or affirmed. Its principles should be understood, questioned, discussed, tested, developed and translated into responsible individual and collective action. This annex proposes the participatory framework through which people may contribute to that continuing work.

Participation is voluntary and must respect human dignity, freedom of conscience, privacy, equality, lawful rights and the legitimacy of reasoned disagreement. No person is required to accept a particular religious, philosophical or political doctrine as a condition of expressing a good-faith question or critique.

## 1. Right to participate

Subject to transparent, proportionate and lawful safeguards, participants may:
- read, study, interpret, question and criticize the Charter;
- create, submit, translate, share and publish knowledge and creative works;
- propose revisions, projects, research questions, standards and practical initiatives;
- collaborate, mentor, review, teach and provide expertise;
- support people, projects and public-interest work through financial or non-financial means;
- participate in consultations, polls and duly constituted collective decisions;
- access the rules governing attribution, recognition, rewards, moderation, appeals and data use.

Access to basic Charter knowledge, good-faith criticism and ordinary participation must not depend on purchasing a token or paying for influence.

## 2. Duties of participation

Participants should respect dignity, privacy, intellectual property, evidence, attribution, accessibility and the right to dissent. They should disclose material conflicts of interest, distinguish fact from opinion and uncertainty, avoid deliberate manipulation, and correct material errors when identified.

## 3. Fair attribution and shared work

Contributions should be recorded with provenance and appropriate attribution. Collaborative work should identify roles and, where appropriate, co-authorship, licensing, permissions and project responsibilities. Sharing a work does not make the sharer its author. Automated or third-party material must be disclosed and used lawfully.

## 4. Deliberation and collective decisions

Discussion, consultation, informal polls and formal referenda are different procedures and must be labeled accordingly. Before a formal process begins, its question, scope, eligibility, method, duration, privacy, audit, conflict-of-interest and appeal rules must be published. A majority result cannot cancel fundamental rights, silence minorities or substitute for lawful public authority.

## 5. Recognition and rewards

The ecosystem may recognize meaningful contributions through transparent non-financial points, contextual reputation signals, awards, grants, compensation or other lawful mechanisms. The rules, criteria, evidence requirements, funding sources, calculation and appeal process must be public and versioned.

Recognition must not be reduced to popularity or volume. Good-faith criticism, peer review, correction, accessibility work, mentoring, research, collaboration and practical outcomes may be recognized. Buying votes, buying reputation, spam, fake accounts and manipulation must not be rewarded.

Participation points, reputation, grants, contractual compensation, treasury assets and token balances are separate categories. No automatic conversion or financial entitlement is implied. No token or wallet is required for basic participation. No reward may purchase agreement, suppress dissent or influence a vote.

## 6. Privacy, identity and participant control

A participant identity service may support authentication, attribution, consent and portable records. Public disclosure of legal identity, sensitive attributes, private activity or wallet relationships must not be presumed. Data collection and identity assurance must be proportionate to the specific risk and purpose. Participants should have clear access, correction, export and appeal procedures, subject to lawful limitations.

## 7. Review, moderation and appeal

Rules must be accessible and consistently applied. Moderation decisions should provide notice and reasons, with a proportionate appeal path and independent review for consequential cases. Content disagreement, criticism of the Charter or dissent from a majority must not by itself be grounds for punishment.

## 8. Revision and public accountability

This annex and its implementing rules remain open to evidence-based revision. Proposed changes should include rationale, affected rights, expected benefits, risks, minority perspectives, implementation costs and review triggers. Each accepted revision must preserve version history and a public explanation.

## 9. Relationship to products and systems

WisdomID, WisdomSpace, WisdomNetwork, WisdomKnowledge, Wisdom Governance & Justice, WisdomEconomy, WisdomChain, WeCoin, WisdomTreasury, WisdomGuard and WisdomAI are supporting systems. Their implementation must remain subordinate to the Charter and applicable law. No product, token, algorithm, administrator or automated decision is the final interpreter of wisdom or human worth.

## 10. Non-activation clause

This annex is a proposal for review. It does not itself create a binding legal contract, activate financial rewards, issue tokens, establish a binding electorate, authorize custody or investment activity, or amend the approved Charter. Each such mechanism requires separate specifications, consultation, legal review, safeguards and explicit approval.
