# Eternal Wisdom Charter / Wisdom Pact

This directory contains the foundational **Eternal Wisdom Charter**, also referred to in its short form as the **Wisdom Pact**.

The master project and ecosystem name remains **Wisdom Intercessor**. “WisdomPact” is the repository name for the Charter and its controlled documentation.

## Document control

All Charter materials are subject to **WI-MR-001 — Master Registry & Document Control**.

Controlled statuses are: Planned, Draft, Review, Approved, Superseded, Archived, Deprecated.

A file existing in this repository does not by itself mean that the document is approved, ratified, or published.

## Official title and language editions

**فارسی:** منشور حکمت جاویدان  
**English:** Eternal Wisdom Charter  
**Short name:** Wisdom Pact

The former word **Global** is not part of the current official title. Legacy filenames containing “Global” are retained for traceability and must not be renamed, moved, or deleted until verified canonical replacements and a migration decision are recorded.

The Persian and English operational editions are intended as parallel controlled texts. Structural correspondence has been documented; full clause-by-clause semantic alignment and rendering verification remain pending.

## Document lineage and edition roles

The proponent-confirmed working lineage is:

1. **Initial / original mother source:** `charter/Eternal Wisdom Charter(Wisdom Pact).pdf`. The source PDF is preserved unchanged; its complete readable text and source-to-operational comparison remain pending.

## Release policy

Charter releases must preserve document identity, version, language alignment, source lineage, revision history, and approval evidence. Superseded or legacy artifacts may be retained under `charter/releases/` for traceability.

GitHub commits are documentation changes; they do not automatically publish the Charter to the public website or activate governance, financial, identity, wallet, or external-service functions.
