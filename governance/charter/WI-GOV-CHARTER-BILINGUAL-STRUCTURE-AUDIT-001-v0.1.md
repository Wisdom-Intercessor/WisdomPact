## 4. Structural details observed

### 4.1 Foundational principles

Both editions identify Principle Zero and Principles 1 through 15. Both include nested thematic provisions under Principles 12, 13, and 14, and subprinciples under Principle 15. The English contents list uses forms such as “Principle 12-1”; the Persian list uses Persian numbering and compound forms such as “اصل ۱-۱۲”.

The Persian and English contents lists show matching titles and ordering for the major nested principle groups inspected. A full clause-by-clause semantic comparison remains outstanding.

### 4.2 Thematic protocols

Both editions list ten thematic protocols, in the same order:
1. Women's rights and gender justice
2. Children's rights
3. Rights of minorities
4. Democracy, rule of law and freedom of expression
5. Economic justice and labor rights
6. Peace, disarmament and security
7. Refugees, migrants and climate-displaced persons
8. Worldwide health and pandemics
9. Cybersecurity, privacy and data sovereignty
10. Bioethics and emerging technologies

### 4.3 Addenda

Both editions list ten addenda, in the same order and with corresponding subject matter:
- Scope, nature and limits of validity
- Position within the constellation of international instruments
- Operational definition of wisdom
- Levels of realizing wisdom
- Monitoring, evaluation and review
- Institution-building and networking
- Invitation to worldwide participation
- Ideological independence
- Evolution and openness
- Digital infrastructure and the Wisdom Ecosystem

The Persian alphabetic labels and English alphabetic labels differ in form, as expected for the respective languages; the sequence and subject headings correspond in the inspected contents.

### 4.4 Closing parts

Both editions contain the Hymn of Praise, Covenant, Technical Appendix, Ten-Year Roadmap, and Part XIII on the presenter's standing and digital infrastructure. The concluding Part XIII in both editions states that the compiler/presenter's standing does not imply a claim of representation, leadership, or political or legal authority.

## 5. Findings and discrepancies requiring attention

### Finding A — Top-level structural alignment

**Result: ALIGNED AT STRUCTURAL LEVEL.** The thirteen top-level parts appear in matching order in both editions.

### Finding B — Principal and protocol inventories

**Result: ALIGNED AT CONTENTS-LIST LEVEL.** The inspected contents lists show corresponding principle groups, ten thematic protocols, and ten addenda. This does not certify every clause's meaning.

### Finding C — Pagination differences

The Persian contents list and English contents list have different page numbers. This is not, by itself, a substantive discrepancy: the editions are separate language layouts. Each edition's own contents and final rendered pagination must be checked against its own PDF/DOCX output.

### Finding D — Extracted-text and layout artifacts

The Persian Markdown file contains PDF-extraction markers such as `<PARSED TEXT FOR PAGE: ...>`, page furniture, and extracted contents text. The English file contains repeated running headers, navigation glyphs, blank lines, and table-like tab-separated content. These artifacts should be normalized only in a controlled canonical transcription task; they must not be silently removed where they may encode source layout or pagination.

### Finding E — Canonical-source policy conflict

The current `charter/README.md` says `CHARTER.md` is reserved for the canonical Markdown representation of the approved Charter and must not be treated as authoritative until the approved source text is imported and verified. Yet the current `CHARTER.md` contains a substantial extracted Persian text of the operational V1.0 edition (57-page extraction markers), rather than an empty placeholder.

This is a document-control and repository-policy inconsistency. It requires an explicit policy decision: either designate these files as operational edition transcriptions with a distinct path/name, or update the canonical-source policy after verification. This audit does not make that decision and does not alter either file.

### Finding F — Initial source PDF not content-verified

The initial source PDF is recorded at `charter/Eternal Wisdom Charter(Wisdom Pact).pdf`, Git blob object ID `ff642d285c7af078dc2c91f5b1575ae4172ede3d`. The available GitHub text retrieval interface could not decode the binary PDF. Its contents have therefore not been compared directly against the V1.0 operational edition in this audit.

## 6. Determination

**Persian/English V1.0 structural alignment: SUPPORTED.** The two editions share the same thirteen-part architecture and corresponding major inventories in the inspected contents.

**Full bilingual semantic equivalence: NOT YET ESTABLISHED.** A clause-by-clause comparison, including definitions, obligations, qualifications, modal verbs, exceptions, addenda, and the Covenant, remains necessary.

**Operational baseline designation: PROPONENT-CONFIRMED.** The proponent's designation of V1.0 (2026) Persian/English as the operational and executable edition is recorded in the preceding lineage clarification.

**Formal ratification/approval: NOT ESTABLISHED.** Neither this audit nor the proponent's document designation constitutes an internal adoption decision or external acceptance.
