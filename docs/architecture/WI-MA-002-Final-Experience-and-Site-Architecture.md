# WI-MA-002 — Final Experience & Site Architecture

**Status:** Final baseline for the current public-site architecture  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Project:** WisdomIntercessor  
**Repository:** WisdomPact

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the final user-facing architecture of the WisdomIntercessor website and its relationship to the underlying Layer 0 + 8 operational architecture.

The website is not modeled as a conventional collection of unrelated pages. Its primary experience is a spatial journey from the **Eternal Wisdom Charter / Wisdom Pact** into three principal spaces:

1. **عالم کبری — Cosmic Realm**
2. **دیوان حکمت — Wisdom Divan**
3. **WisdomCore — Personal Wisdom Core**

The three spaces are experience surfaces. They are not replacements for the logical architectural layers.

## 2. Entry sequence

The public entry sequence is:

**رستاخیز حکمت جاویدان → منشور حکمت جاویدان → درگاه ورود → سه فضای اصلی منظومه**

The published post **رستاخیز حکمت جاویدان** is the opening declaration and gateway. It presents the Eternal Wisdom Charter / Wisdom Pact and ends with navigation into the three principal spaces.

## 3. Three-space experience model

### 3.1 عالم کبری — Cosmic Realm

The first space after entering the Wisdom System is the cosmic/world view.

Primary experience:

- Milky Way / cosmic scale
- Solar-system scale
- Earth as the primary living focal object
- progressive zoom and focus
- geographic exploration
- time and place context
- day/night and seasonal layers
- future connection to live environmental, temporal and spatial data
- gateway from the macrocosmic world into the microcosmic Wisdom System

The Cosmic Realm is the visual and experiential representation of **عالَم کبری**. It must not be reduced to a static hero image in the mature implementation.

### 3.2 دیوان حکمت — Wisdom Divan

The Wisdom Divan is the central governance and coordination space of the ecosystem.

It contains **eight Wisdom Halls**:

1. **WisdomNetwork** — تالار شبکه و ارتباطات حکمت
2. **WisdomChain** — تالار زنجیره و مبادلات حکمت
3. **WisdomTreasury** — تالار خزانه و منابع حکمت
4. **WisdomKnowledge** — تالار دانش و آفرینش حکمت
5. **WisdomGuard** — تالار پاسداری و امنیت حکمت
6. **Wisdom Governance & Justice** — تالار حکمرانی و عدالت حکمت
7. **WisdomLife & Health** — تالار زندگی و سلامت حکمت
8. **Wisdom of Existence & Time** — تالار هستی و زمان حکمت

The halls are human-facing governance domains. They are not merely menu labels.

### 3.3 WisdomCore

WisdomCore is the user's personal operational center and interface to the ecosystem.

It provides eight service/tool domains corresponding to the major operational concerns:

1. Communications / Network
2. Knowledge
3. Resources / Treasury
4. Economy / Chain
5. Security / Guard
6. Governance
7. Life & Health
8. Existence & Time

The eight WisdomCore service layers are related to the eight Divan Halls but are **not the same architectural object**:

- **Divan Halls = governance/coordination domains**
- **WisdomCore layers = personal services, tools and workflows**

WisdomCore must support problems and opportunities ranging from personal matters to collective, environmental, civilizational and cosmic-scale concerns.

## 4. WisdomSpace and personal showcase

Every participant should have a personal workspace inside WisdomCore.

That workspace can expose a public or selectively shared **WisdomSpace / Personal Showcase**.

The showcase is a first-class participation capability, not a decorative profile page. It can contain:

- identity and profile
- authored works
- knowledge and research
- projects
- skills
- services
- products
- resources or assets offered
- collaborations
- community participation
- selected records and achievements

The model is therefore:

**WisdomID → WisdomCore → WisdomSpace → Personal Showcase**

WisdomSpace does not replace the three-space experience or the Layer 0 + 8 operational architecture.

## 5. Logical architecture remains Layer 0 + 8

The underlying system architecture remains:

- **Layer 0 — WisdomCore**
- **Layer 1 — WisdomNetwork**
- **Layer 2 — WisdomChain**
- **Layer 3 — WisdomTreasury**
- **Layer 4 — WisdomKnowledge**
- **Layer 5 — WisdomGuard**
- **Layer 6 — Wisdom Governance & Justice**
- **Layer 7 — WisdomLife & Health**
- **Layer 8 — Wisdom of Existence & Time**

The website experience is therefore a projection of the architecture, not a replacement for it.

## 6. Concept / service / product boundary

The architecture preserves a strict distinction:

- **Concept:** an idea, model, principle or future possibility.
- **Service:** an operational capability delivered to a participant.
- **Product:** a defined user-facing deliverable with a lifecycle.

A Hall can contain concepts, services and products. A WisdomCore layer can expose services and tools from one or more operational domains.

## 7. Open architecture principles

The architecture remains:

- open-source by design
- vendor-neutral
- interoperable
- community-extensible
- versioned and documented
- separable into content, software, data and governance layers

WordPress is the current public CMS/application surface. It is not the complete long-term architecture.

## 8. Current website implementation

The public WordPress implementation currently exposes:

- **Opening:** رستاخیز حکمت جاویدان
- **Cosmic Realm:** /fa/عالم-کبری-cosmic-realm/
- **Wisdom Divan:** /fa/دیوان-حکمت-wisdom-divan/
- **WisdomCore:** /fa/wisdomcore-میزکار-حکمت/

The current pages implement the experience shell and navigation model. Live 3D planetary data, real-time environmental feeds and deeper application services remain implementation layers to be connected behind these stable experience boundaries.

## 9. Navigation principle

The primary journey is not a conventional top-level product menu.

The intended journey is:

**Charter → Cosmic Realm → Wisdom Divan → WisdomCore → WisdomSpace**

Users may move between the three principal spaces at any time.

## 10. Architectural rule

No future feature should introduce a fourth competing primary experience space without an explicit architecture decision.

New capabilities should normally enter one of:

- Cosmic Realm as world/time/place context
- Wisdom Divan as governance/coordination
- WisdomCore as personal service/tooling
- WisdomSpace as participant-owned interactive presence
