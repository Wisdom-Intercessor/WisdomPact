# WI-MA-001 — Master Architecture & System Structure

**Project:** WisdomIntercessor  
**Repository:** WisdomPact  
**Status:** Draft / Architectural Baseline  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## 1. Purpose

WI-MA-001 defines the master structural model of WisdomIntercessor. It is an architectural baseline, not a complete implementation specification.

The architecture separates the project's civilizational vision from individual products and technologies. Technology is an implementation layer serving the defined objectives rather than the definition of the project itself.

## 2. Core Identity

**WisdomIntercessor** is the master name of the project, website, and ecosystem.

**WisdomPact** is the foundational pact/charter-oriented repository and document domain within the wider ecosystem.

**عالم صغری** is the conceptual digital/virtual project space.

**عالم کبری** refers to the real world and the larger field of human, terrestrial, temporal, and cosmic existence.

The digital environment is treated as a representation and extension of real-world participation, not as a replacement for the real world.

## 3. Mission Model

The project is organized around the principle of increasing the freedom, availability, connection, and application of wisdom in human and systemic affairs.

The architecture therefore supports:

- individual participation;
- collective knowledge and collaboration;
- cultural and creative production;
- economic exchange;
- knowledge and learning;
- health and life services;
- security and justice;
- governance and collective decision processes;
- environmental and broader existence-related concerns.

These are architectural domains. They are not claims that all capabilities already exist.

## 4. Architectural Layers

### Layer 0 — WisdomCore

The central coordination and personal/organizational workspace layer.

Primary functions include identity, orientation, personal dashboard, participation context, and access to ecosystem capabilities.

### Layer 1 — WisdomNetwork

Communication, media, community, publishing, and discovery.

**WisdomHub** is the media/community concept within this layer. Media tools remain services or components unless separately defined as products.

### Layer 2 — WisdomChain

Economic and communication infrastructure, transaction records, interoperability, and future decentralized components where justified.

Blockchain is an implementation option, not an architectural objective by itself.

### Layer 3 — WisdomTreasury

The resource, contribution, allocation, and collective-wealth domain.

The conceptual metaphor **جام جم** belongs here.

### Layer 4 — WisdomKnowledge

Knowledge, education, research, experimentation, simulation, and experience.

**WisdomPlay** belongs conceptually to this layer as a learning/experience domain. Individual games or simulations require separate specifications.

### Layer 5 — WisdomGuard

Participatory safety, protection, resilience, and public-security capabilities, subject to law, privacy, due process, and human oversight.

### Layer 6 — WisdomGovernance & Justice

Participation, governance, justice, accountability, transparency, dispute processes, and collective decision architecture.

The conceptual metaphor **تخت حکمت / تخت جمشید** belongs here.

### Layer 7 — WisdomLife

Health, wellbeing, daily-life support, and related services.

**WisdomHealth** and **WisdomFit** are architectural concepts at this stage, not declarations that operational services are already deployed.

### Layer 8 — WisdomExistence & Time

Long-horizon, environmental, temporal, existential, and cosmic-scale information and participation.

## 5. Participant-Centered Model

The ecosystem should allow a participant to maintain:

- a Wisdom ID;
- a personal/work desk;
- a presentation or showcase space;
- knowledge, creative, professional, or commercial contributions;
- participation and collaboration records;
- appropriate access to ecosystem services.

The architecture must not require every participant to become a formal organization or business.

## 6. Direct Participation Principle

Where technically, legally, and operationally appropriate, participants should be able to create, publish, offer, discover, receive, and collaborate on content, knowledge, works, and services without unnecessary intermediaries.

Intermediation may still be required for safety, legal compliance, payments, verification, dispute resolution, or other legitimate controls.

## 7. Identity and Access

Wisdom ID is the intended identity layer.

WisdomWallet is initially treated as an optional interaction/transaction capability rather than a mandatory standalone product.

Identity, authentication, authorization, privacy, and auditability must be specified independently in technical architecture documents.

## 8. Concept / Service / Product Separation

Every future feature must be classified before implementation:

### Concept
Explains what an idea means and why it exists.

### Service
Defines an operational capability available to participants.

### Product
Defines a concrete offering with scope, owner, lifecycle, requirements, and release criteria.

This separation is mandatory for architecture governance.

## 9. Repository Role

WisdomPact serves as a controlled knowledge and foundational-document repository.

It must not become an unstructured archive of every idea.

The repository should progressively contain:

1. controlled foundational documents;
2. architecture specifications;
3. technical specifications;
4. UX and experience specifications;
5. implementation specifications;
6. research and evidence;
7. historical decisions and revisions.

## 10. Public Website Boundary

The GitHub repository and wisdomintercessor.com are related but distinct layers.

GitHub is the controlled development/documentation environment.

The website is the public experience and service surface.

A repository commit does not automatically authorize website publication.

## 11. Architectural Constraints

The architecture must:

- preserve traceability;
- avoid premature productization;
- avoid dependency on a single technology vendor;
- distinguish experimental components from production systems;
- protect participant data;
- support incremental implementation;
- permit future replacement of individual technical components;
- preserve historical versions of important decisions.

## 12. Initial Implementation Sequence

The initial sequence is:

1. Master Registry and document control;
2. Master Architecture;
3. Technical Architecture;
4. UX / Outdoor / Indoor / WisdomSpace architecture;
5. foundational Charter/Pact documents;
6. implementation specifications;
7. controlled deployment.
