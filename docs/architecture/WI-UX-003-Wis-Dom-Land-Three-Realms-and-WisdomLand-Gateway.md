## 1. Purpose and precedence

This specification establishes the canonical public-site page model around the three realms and preserves the established **Wis / Dom / Land** sequence and **WisdomLand** entry gateway.

It clarifies and supersedes earlier website experience mappings wherever they conflict with this page model. It does not delete or invalidate the Layer 0 + 8 logical architecture, the eight Wisdom domains, the WisdomCore operational concept, WisdomSpace, or the historical records of earlier designs. Those remain distinct architectural dimensions and must be mapped into this model rather than substituted for it.

## 2. Governing philosophy

The human being lives and acts across three interdependent realms:

1. **عالم درونی — Wis:** the inner realm of the human being: self-knowledge, consciousness, identity, learning, wisdom, conscience, judgment, intention, and personal agency.
2. **عالم پیونددهنده — Dom:** the connecting realm: relationships, communication, community, knowledge exchange, institutions, economy, governance, law, culture, technology, and the systems through which people interact and coordinate.
3. **عالم بیرونی — Land:** the outer realm: Earth, nature, living systems, geography, environment, resources, time, space, and the wider cosmos in which human life unfolds.

The three realms are not isolated territories or mutually exclusive subject categories. They form a continuous human-world relationship:

**Inner human (Wis) ↔ Connecting systems and relationships (Dom) ↔ Outer world and existence (Land).**

The human being is the conscious participant and responsible agent moving through all three. The connecting realm mediates interaction; it does not replace either the human or the outer world.

## 3. WisdomLand gateway

**WisdomLand** is the unified entry gateway to the three realms. It is the portal, orientation and navigation layer, not a fourth realm and not a replacement for Wis, Dom or Land.

The gateway must:
- explain the three-realm philosophy in clear language;
- orient visitors before entry;
- provide direct entry to Wis, Dom and Land;
- expose the Eternal Wisdom Charter as the guiding framework;
- provide language, accessibility, search and document access;
- preserve clear return paths between realms.

The gateway must not imply that all services are already operational. Each capability must carry an honest state such as Planned, Prototype, In Development or Operational.

## 4. Canonical site/page structure

| Sequence | Canonical name | Realm / role | Primary purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gateway | **WisdomLand** | Entry portal | Orientation, Charter, navigation and entry |
| Page 1 | **Wis** | Inner realm / عالم درونی | Human identity, self-knowledge, wisdom and personal agency |
| Page 2 | **Dom** | Connecting realm / عالم پیونددهنده | Relationships, networks, institutions, knowledge, technology and coordination |
| Page 3 | **Land** | Outer realm / عالم بیرونی | Earth, nature, environment, geography, time, space and cosmos |

The short names **Wis**, **Dom**, and **Land** must remain stable in navigation, URLs where already established, page identifiers and design references. Persian realm names remain visible and primary in Persian-language experience.

## 5. Charter role across all three realms

The **Eternal Wisdom Charter / منشور حکمت جاویدان** is the guiding normative and reflective framework across all three realms. It is not confined to a single page or a single realm.

The Charter should help people:
- understand principles and responsibilities relevant to the inner realm;
- evaluate relationships, institutions, systems and collective decisions in the connecting realm;
- consider impacts on Earth, nature, living beings, resources and future generations in the outer realm;
- understand interactions and trade-offs across all three realms;
- deliberate, question, criticize, propose revisions and review outcomes.

The Charter is a guide, not an instrument of coercion. It must not claim infallibility, monopolize truth, override human rights, impose religious or philosophical belief, or replace lawful due process and accountable institutions. It must remain open to evidence, pluralism, reasoned disagreement, transparent revision and public scrutiny.

## 6. Relationship to the existing architecture

The three realms are a conceptual and user-experience map. They are not replacements for:
- **Layer 0 + 8:** the logical architecture and eight principal operational domains;
- **WisdomCore:** the cross-ecosystem core and participant operational capabilities;
- **WisdomSpace:** the participant's personal workspace or selectively shared presence;
- **Wisdom Tree:** the knowledge and architecture taxonomy;
- **WQIE:** the evidence-aware model for typed relations and cross-domain analysis.

Each Layer 0/8 component, product, document and workflow may support one or more realms. A single item may have a primary realm placement and additional cross-realm links. No forced one-to-one mapping is permitted.

## 7. WQIE three-realm analysis requirement

WQIE records and analytical reports should be able to identify:
- affected realm(s): INNER / CONNECTING / OUTER;
- affected people, groups, institutions, ecosystems or other relevant entities;
- direct and indirect effects within each realm;
- cross-realm dependencies, feedback, synergies, conflicts and trade-offs;
- temporal scope, uncertainty, evidence and provenance;
- rights, privacy, inclusion, reversibility and future-generation considerations.

A realm label is a classification aid, not a causal conclusion or normative score. Unknown effects remain unknown; they must not be represented as zero. Quantification requires an explicit method and defensible evidence.

## 8. Page design and navigation requirements

### WisdomLand gateway
- concise statement of the three-realm philosophy;
- Charter entry and explanation of its guiding role;
- three distinct entry paths to Wis, Dom and Land;
- visible relationship between the three realms;
- current implementation status and accessible alternatives.

### Wis — Inner realm
- personhood, identity and dignity;
- self-knowledge, cognition, learning and wisdom;
- conscience, ethics, rights, freedoms and responsibilities;
- personal development, agency and reflective practice;
- tools and services must remain optional and privacy-respecting.

### Dom — Connecting realm
- interpersonal and community relationships;
- communication and knowledge exchange;
- networks, institutions and public participation;
- economy, governance, justice and coordination;
- digital infrastructure, data and technology;
- transparency, accountability, safety and dispute resolution.

### Land — Outer realm
- Earth, geography and place;
- nature, biodiversity and living systems;
- climate, environment and sustainability;
- resources, built environments and human impact;
- time, space and cosmic context;
- responsibility toward future generations.

The lists are navigational scopes, not rigid disciplinary boundaries. Cross-realm content must be linked rather than duplicated without context.

## 9. Design and interaction principles

- Present the three realms as one connected journey, not three unrelated microsites.
- Preserve a consistent navigation system and clear realm identity.
- Use Persian as the primary language and English as the secondary language.
- Ensure mobile, desktop and accessible non-visual navigation.
- Distinguish real data and operational functions from illustrative visuals, prototypes and future plans.
- Avoid claiming live cosmic, environmental or social data unless the relevant data source and update status are verified.
- Keep the gateway lightweight enough to serve as a reliable entry point.

## 10. Governance and change control

This document is a proposed baseline until reviewed and merged through the repository's normal process. Changes to realm definitions, the WisdomLand gateway role, page sequence, or Charter authority require a documented rationale and impact review across site architecture, UX, Charter, WQIE and related specifications.

No production WordPress page, menu, homepage, URL or published content is changed merely by merging this repository document. Website implementation and publication remain separately controlled actions.
