# Architecture Decision Record — 2026-09-25

## Decision

The WisdomIntercessor website adopts a three-space primary experience architecture:

1. **عالم کبری / Cosmic Realm**
2. **دیوان حکمت / Wisdom Divan**
3. **WisdomCore**

The opening public artifact remains **رستاخیز حکمت جاویدان / Eternal Wisdom Charter / Wisdom Pact**.

## Rationale

The three spaces separate three distinct user questions:

- What is happening in the world and existence?
- How are shared affairs governed and coordinated?
- What can I, as a participant, do, create, manage and contribute?

The eight Divan Halls represent governance domains. The eight WisdomCore layers represent personal services, tools and workflows. They are related but must not be conflated.

The personal participant showcase, **WisdomSpace**, remains a first-class capability within WisdomCore.

## Consequences

- The site is not organized as a conventional blog or product catalog.
- The logical Layer 0 + 8 architecture remains intact.
- WordPress can implement the initial experience without becoming the long-term architectural boundary.
- Future live data, 3D rendering, identity, service and application layers can be connected behind stable experience boundaries.
