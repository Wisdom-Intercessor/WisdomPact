# عالَم صغری / دیوان حکمت

The digital/civilizational realm presented through eight Wisdom Halls corresponding to L1–L8.

1. WisdomNetwork
2. WisdomChain
3. WisdomTreasury
4. WisdomKnowledge
5. WisdomGuard
6. Governance & Justice
7. Life & Health
8. Existence & Time

A hall is an architectural/navigation surface; its existence does not by itself prove that every service beneath it is deployed.
