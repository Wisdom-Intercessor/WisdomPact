# WI-MR-001 — Master Registry

Status: STRUCTURE-ONLY / awaiting final specifications
Version: 0.1-draft

## Purpose
Authoritative registry for the Wisdom Intercessor ecosystem. This registry separates architectural layers, products, services, concepts, institutions, standards and future proposals.

## Current canonical model
- Meta Layer: Layer 0 — WisdomCore / human sovereignty, wisdom and orchestration
- Operational Layers: 1–8
- Current product baseline: 52 items
- Layer 8: Global Community — products 43–52

## Registry rules
1. A registry entry does not imply implementation, legal incorporation, operational availability or commercial launch.
2. A product may later be reclassified as protocol, service, institution, program, standard or concept without losing its registry ID.
3. Final specifications are stored separately and linked from this registry.
4. Empty specification slots are intentional and must not be filled by inference.
